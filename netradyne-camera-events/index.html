Camera events, driver by driver

Every Netradyne camera event now lands in Warrior Ops on its own — pinned to the van and the driver who had it that day, with a map of where it keeps happening and a snapshot you can send the driver in two clicks.

Live now Managers & owners Five-minute setup

Open Safety → Netradyne and you can see who to coach, what for, and where — without opening Netradyne.

What it's for

  • Knowing who to coach first. Drivers are ranked by how serious their events were, not just how many — Netradyne's own "severe" events count three times.
  • Putting a name on "Unknown Driver". When Netradyne doesn't know who was driving, the van and the rota fill it in — and say so, so nobody is blamed blind.
  • Briefing the danger spots. The map shows where the same thing keeps happening — a junction, a perimeter road — ready to copy into the morning huddle or WhatsApp.
  • Sending a driver their own evidence. A one-page picture of their events and the one thing to work on, by email or straight into WhatsApp.

How it works

01

Set it up once on your computer

Safety → Netradyne → Download the Chrome extension. Unzip it, open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked and pick the folder. Pin it. Then delete the download — it contains your company's private key.

02

Press Import now, log in to Netradyne once

Click the extension → Import now. It opens its own pinned Netradyne tab; log in there if it asks. The first import brings in the last 84 days in about a minute. From then on it runs itself every 20 minutes while Chrome is open.

03

Start with "Needs you"

The strip at the top lists what needs a person — the worst driver today, the worst spot on the map, drivers to link, events that couldn't be pinned. Click a chip and it takes you straight there. Switch between Today, Day −1 and Lifetime, or pick a site.

04

Brief the hotspots

Under Where it happens, the heatmap shows where events cluster. Zoom into a street to see each event — a white ring means we know whose it was; click one for when and who. Copy briefing puts the top five spots, with map links, on your clipboard.

05

Coach the driver

In Coach first, click a driver: every event by day and van, and the one thing to work on. Copy image for WhatsApp, Download PNG, Show on map — or Email to driver (owners only; it asks you to confirm the address before anything is sent).

What changes for you

Station manager

No more screenshots from Netradyne. Work the Needs you strip, brief the hotspots, open the top drivers. If a driver shows "Not linked", pick them from the list once and it sticks.

Drivers

Nothing automatic. They only hear about it when a manager sends them their snapshot — no emails go out by themselves.

Owners

Only owners can email a driver their snapshot. Keys for the extension are listed on the page and can be revoked at any time.

Looks wrong, but is correct

Our total is a few higher than Netradyne's Drivers page

Netradyne's Drivers page hides deactivated drivers unless you tick "Include Deactivated Drivers". We keep their events — they happened. Tick the box and the numbers match.

A driver has events "via rota"

Netradyne didn't know who was driving (its "Unknown Driver"), so we used the van's VIN and the rota for that day. If the rota was wrong, the name will be wrong — fix the rota and it corrects itself.

A driver shows "Rota differs"

Netradyne named this driver, but the rota had someone else in that van. The event stays on the driver Netradyne named — the note is there so you can check.

The map shows yesterday when I'm on Today

Netradyne only releases event locations once a day is finished. Today's spots appear tomorrow morning. Everything else on the page is live.

Most dots on the map have no name

Netradyne gives a location without a driver. We only put a name on a dot when theirs was the only van with that kind of event that day — most red lights, phone use and harsh braking; never seatbelt. The rest still show where it happens.

The fleet GreenZone score changed during the week

It's the week so far — it says "3 days so far" underneath — and it settles when the week ends, exactly as on Netradyne's dashboard.

Camera button presses aren't in the totals

On purpose. That's the driver recording something, not a violation. You'll still see them in the driver's detail.

The extension icon shows a "!"

It needs you — almost always Netradyne has logged you out. Log in again in the pinned Netradyne tab; the next import catches up everything you missed.

Where the limits are

Known and deliberate

  • It reads Netradyne and changes nothing there. It needs Chrome open on a computer that's awake, logged in to Netradyne — imports pause when the laptop sleeps and catch up after.
  • Netradyne only lets us go back about 84 days. "Lifetime" means everything since your first import, and it grows from here.
  • No driver is emailed automatically. Every snapshot is sent by a person.
  • A van only matches if its VIN is in Van Tracker. Events on a van we don't know are listed under "Not pinned to a driver" — never dropped.
