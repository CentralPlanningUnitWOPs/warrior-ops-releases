Photograph the van before it goes out

A hire van is now walked round and photographed before it can go on the road, and photographed again the day it goes back — the same angles, in the same order. When a hire company invoices you months later, the answer is two pictures of the same panel.

Live now Station managers Built for a phone

A van you add is Incomplete until somebody has walked round it with a camera — and that walk-round is the evidence you argue with later.

What it's for

  • Answering an invoice that arrives six months after the van went back. The van's folder holds every photograph from the day it arrived and the day it left, so a charge for a panel is either fair or it is visibly not.
  • Knowing what came with the van. Spare wheel, tyre pump, wheel-changing kit, first aid kit, door seal, lock and wheel nut — recorded on arrival, and asked for again on the way out.
  • Finding the trackers on the way out. Whoever fits one photographs where it went, so the person removing it months later is not searching a dashboard.
  • Replacing the shared spreadsheet. Every hire term now lives on an On/Off Hire tab in the van tracker, across all sites, with the evidence behind each registration one click away.

How it works

1

You add the van as normal

It appears as Incomplete and is not on the road yet. That is not a fault — it means nobody has photographed it.

2

It asks what shape the van is

Panel van or Luton. It decides what you get asked for: a Luton needs its box and tail-lift, a panel van needs its sliding door.

3

You walk round it, taking the shots it names

25 angles for a panel van, 27 for a Luton. It names one at a time, tells you what has to be in frame and why that panel gets charged for, opens the camera on a tap, and moves to the next as soon as you have taken it.

4

Then the questions, in four short groups

Kit, tracker, compliance, damage. It only asks what applies — say no tracker is fitted and it never asks you where it is.

5

The van goes on the road

Once the folder is complete you put it on the road from the last screen. Months later, handing it back walks the same list in the same order, showing you the arrival photograph of each panel beside the camera so you can match it.

What changes for you

Station managers

The real change. Adding a van is now a ten-minute walk round it with a phone, and the van will not go on the road until that is done. Handing one back is the same walk again.

Drivers

Nothing changes. This is not in the driver app and nobody is asked to photograph anything on shift.

Management

The On/Off Hire tab replaces the shared sheet and covers every site. Click a registration to see that van's whole history — every hire, its checklist, its photographs, and any damage recorded against it in the app.

Looks wrong, but is correct

A van I just added says "Incomplete"

That is the van telling you it has not been photographed yet. It is not off-hired and nothing has gone wrong — it simply cannot go on the road until the walk-round is finished. Open it and it picks up where you left off.

Twenty-five photographs seems like a lot

It is the point. Every angle on that list is a panel somebody could invoice you for. One missing shot is the one they charge for. The camera opens straight from the tap and moves on the moment you have taken it, so the set takes a walk round the van rather than a morning.

It never asked me where the tracker is

Because you answered no to trackers fitted. It is a question about a tracker that exists — there is nothing to photograph. Change that answer to yes and the question comes straight back.

The hand-back never asked about the spare wheel

Because it was recorded as absent when the van arrived. If it was missing on the way in, you are not asked to hand it back. That is deliberate, and the on-hire answer is still in the folder to show why.

I pressed the last button and it said "Uploading 3…"

Photographs upload in the background while you carry on shooting, so most are already sent by the time you finish. If the last few are still going, that button waits for them — the evidence has to actually arrive before the van is cleared.

My photo count went down

One did not upload, so it was removed from the list rather than left ticked. A tick for a photograph sitting in a phone is worse than no tick. There is a Retry at the bottom — one tap sends everything that failed.

A Luton asked me for more shots than the van before it

Correct. A Luton adds the box from both sides and the tail-lift, and drops the sliding door a panel van has. 27 against 25, at both ends of the hire.

Where the limits are

Known and deliberate

  • The archive starts now. The old sheet's history is not in here — only vans on-hired through the app have folders, so keep the sheet for anything handed back before this week.
  • There is no way to skip the walk-round from the app. A van that genuinely cannot be photographed cannot be put on the road this way — tell Dan rather than working around it.
  • Photographs are kept for 24 months after the van goes back, then deleted automatically. That is double the six months Dan flagged as the risk window.
  • ClearQuote and KITT are separate systems. The checklist records that each was done; it does not do either of them for you.
  • Damage recorded here is damage that arrived with the van. Damage we cause on shift is still the existing damage process, untouched.
