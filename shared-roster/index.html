Now live · 16 September 2026

Drivers can work two sites

Two sites can now share some of their drivers, in both directions, without anyone being moved between them. A day booked at one site shows greyed on the other, so neither site can take a day the other has already planned.

Live now Station managers Owner switches it on

Set it up once, and swapping a driver between sites stops meaning an edit to their profile.

What it’s for

  • Putting a driver on either site’s rota, any week. Two days here and two there is now an ordinary week, not a series of profile changes.
  • Seeing what the other site has already planned. Their booked days show on your grid with their site’s code on them, so you are working from the same picture they are.
  • Stopping two sites booking the same driver on one day. A day belongs to whoever booked it, and the other site cannot take it by accident.
  • Standing a second site up. Share a handful of people while its own team is being built, and stop sharing when it no longer needs to.

How it works

01

An owner says two sites share drivers

Under Stations, in “Sites that share drivers”. Done once. Until this happens nothing in the app looks any different.

02

A manager ticks who is in the pool

A Shared drivers button appears on the Drivers screen. Both sites’ people are listed side by side; tick anyone who should be available to both. There is no end date and no need to come back and untick them for a week they stay at home.

03

Either site books them on a free day

A shared driver sits in their home site’s normal roster, and in the visiting section at the other. Any day nobody has booked is open to both.

04

The day locks at the other site

The moment one site books it, the other sees that day greyed with the booking site’s code on it. It cannot be clicked or overwritten.

05

To hand a day over, clear it

Set the day to Off and it opens up at both sites again. That is the whole hand-over: no request, no approval, no waiting.

What changes for you

Station manager

Roster shared drivers straight from your own grid. No profile edits, no asking anyone to send them back. Grey days with another site’s code are theirs — ring them if you need one.

Drivers

Their weekly confirm email now names both sites and the link shows which days are at which. They get one email, not one from each site. The day-before confirm is unchanged and still comes from wherever they are working that day.

Management

Pay is unchanged. A driver’s days land on the pay sheet of the site where the work actually happened, so a split week appears on both sheets and adds up to the days worked.

Looks wrong, but is correct

Nothing looks any different

Then no two sites have been paired yet. This is off until an owner switches it on under Stations, and every screen behaves exactly as it did before. That is deliberate — nobody’s rota changes underneath them.

A grey cell won’t let me click it

The other site has that day. Nobody can take a day another site has already planned — that is the point of it. They clear the day at their end and it opens up at yours.

The whole week is grey — I can’t book them at all

Their other site has them booked every day. Correct, and worth a phone call: they have to free a day before you can have one. The app will not quietly take one off them.

I put them on Holiday but the other site still can’t book them

Right, and on purpose. Holiday is not availability. Somebody on holiday should not be picked up by the other site. Only Off frees a day — the same goes for standby, which means you are holding them.

I cleared a day but the other site still shows it as mine

Give it a few seconds and refresh. The rota holds what it last loaded for a short while, so flipping straight between two sites can show you the previous picture. Nothing is stuck — it just has not caught up yet.

A blank day — is that offered to me, or their day off?

The screen cannot tell you, and does not pretend to. A free day looks the same either way. This is the bit that still needs a conversation between the two managers, and it is meant to.

They’re in the main list at one site and at the bottom at the other

As intended. A driver keeps one home site and stays in its normal roster all week, badged with where else they work. At the other site they appear in the visiting section. One driver, one roster, two grids.

Where the limits are

Known and deliberate

  • Nothing hands a day over on its own. One site clears it, the other books it — the app never takes a day from anybody.
  • A free day does not say whether it is genuinely offered. The two managers still talk; this shows the plan, it does not make it.
  • Holiday and standby never free a day. Only Off does.
  • A driver still works one shift a day, as always. Sharing changes where they can be booked, never how often.
  • Stopping is instant and costs nothing: unpair the two sites and both rotas go back to normal. Nobody loses their place on a roster.
